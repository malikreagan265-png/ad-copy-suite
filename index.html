Why platform-native matters
Google Ads is keyword-intent matching. Meta is scroll-stopping. LinkedIn is credibility-first. Each has a different creative grammar — and a different way of failing.
A Meta primary text that opens with "Most planners sit on a shelf" stops a scroll. The same line in a Google headline gets cut off at 30 characters and fails. A LinkedIn ad that opens with a professional pain point earns a click from a marketing director. The same line on Meta reads as jargon. The Ad Copy Suite generates for each platform's actual requirements — not the same copy shortened to fit.
